Becca
Portfolio

Personal project · AI · inspired by

An AIwith a soul

I recreated Rebecca from Cyberpunk: Edgerunners as a real-time voice AI. Every reaction, emotional state and even her voice were rebuilt to stay as close as possible to the anime character, with memory of what I tell her and voice-controlled PC automation.

Why I built it

Inspired by VTubers: characters with their own avatar, voice and personality, chatting live. I wanted to see if an AI could be a character like that, all on its own, on my PC.

The experience I wanted to create

Talking to a character, not an assistant: someone who recognizes your voice, remembers what you told her and reacts with real emotion.

01The character

Recreating Rebecca

Recreating someone everyone recognizes is much harder than making up some generic assistant.

I chose Rebecca from Cyberpunk: Edgerunners because she's an iconic character with a one-of-a-kind personality. If a reply comes out generic, fans notice right away.

The voice was another challenge: it needed her tone and emotion, with laughter mid-sentence, not a text-to-speech reader.

02A conversation, step by step

From what I say to what she does

  1. 1 · Listens

    I speak

    Whisper, running on the GPU, turns speech into text and checks whether the voice is mine.

  2. 2 · Thinks

    She decides what to say

    The LLM gets what I said along with her current mood, affinity level and saved memories.

  3. 3 · Replies

    Talks while she thinks

    Each finished sentence is voiced right away, without waiting for the full reply, with real emotion and laughter along the way.

  4. 4 · Reacts

    Her body follows

    The VRM avatar changes expressions and animations based on how she's feeling.

  5. 5 · Acts

    If it's a request, she does it

    She opens games, plays music on Spotify and YouTube, sets reminders and handles small tasks in Windows.

03Technical evolution

What's inside

  1. 01

    Real-time conversation

    Speech-to-text, LLM and voice synthesis in a single streaming pipeline: each finished sentence is voiced right away, without waiting for the full reply.

  2. 02

    Knowing who's talking

    Speaker identification on top of Whisper: she knows when the voice isn't mine and reacts to it.

  3. 03

    Emotions that stick

    Mood, jealousy and affinity are states that shift with every conversation and get saved, along with memories of what matters.

  4. 04

    Voice and body in sync

    The emotion behind each reply sets the tone of her voice and the VRM avatar's expression, so what she says, how she sounds and what she shows all match.
