Product · SaaS for aesthetic clinics

BeautyExp.

One system for the entire aesthetic clinic: scheduling, patients, protocols, before-and-after photos and finances, with AI that organizes patient records and suggests treatments. On desktop and mobile.

Why I built it

Practitioners spend years learning to care for skin, and then their day disappears into WhatsApp, spreadsheets, sticky notes for follow-ups and photos lost on their phone.

The experience I wanted to create

Open the system and know exactly what to do: who to confirm, who to charge, which slots are open. The technology handles the routine; she takes care of people.

01The challenge

Truly simple

Clinic software is usually one giant form. Beauty had to be the opposite.

Every screen shows a summary and the next step with a clear button, like "Reschedule" or "Send invoice", instead of a generic form. Related screens share the same layout, so once you learn one, you know them all.

So no one gets lost, the system itself has video lessons for every task.

02A day at the clinic

From booking to results

  1. Morning

    What needs attention

    "My Day" lists who to confirm for tomorrow and who has a pending payment, with a button for each action.

  2. Schedule

    Open slots show up on their own

    Gaps in the day become a shortcut to book, no hunting through the calendar.

  3. New patient

    Write it your way

    Name, date of birth, phone and main complaint in one block of text: the AI splits it into fields and the practitioner just double-checks.

  4. Appointment

    AI suggests the protocol

    Based on the complaint, protocols from the clinic's library appear ranked by fit, with sessions, intervals and duration.

  5. Results

    Before and after, side by side

    Photos from every session are stored in the patient's record, with a slider to compare progress.

  6. End of the month

    No spreadsheets

    Revenue received, pending and overdue, all on one dashboard.

03Technical evolution

What's inside

  1. 01

    Web and app with the same look

    React + Vite in the browser and React Native with Expo on mobile, sharing theme tokens generated from a single source and checks that every route exists on both.

  2. 02

    NestJS API

    Back end with CQRS and JWT, a cloud Postgres database (Neon) and migrations applied on deploy.

  3. 03

    AI where it saves typing

    Patient records built from free text and protocol suggestions based on the complaint, always confirmed by the practitioner.

  4. 04

    A demo that never gets stale

    A sample clinic is generated with dates relative to today, so the schedule looks real in any presentation.

  5. 05

    Video lessons

    Tutorials recorded straight from the system, with cursor and narration, for every everyday task.

  6. 06

    A pitch built in code

    The pitch video was built in HTML and rendered frame by frame, with the real screens rebuilt.
