PaperHallway

A pencil-drawn hallway where every door opens a part of my résumé.

Why I built it

It was the first version of my résumé: a test of whether it could be a place to walk around, with doors and rooms, instead of a long page to scroll.

The experience I wanted to create

The calm of flipping through a sketchbook that comes to life: pencil lines, folded paper and a surprise behind every door.

01The original idea

A hand-drawn hallway

The reference was Itom's website: a clean, hand-drawn hallway with doors that open onto other worlds.

I studied how it guides visitors (the facade at the entrance, doors that light up with color on hover, a title that changes as you explore) and built my own with my content.

02How it grew

From sketch to paper

  1. Entry

    Facade and doors

    The entrance is the front of a house. The doors are gray sketches that take on color on hover and open when clicked.

  2. Rooms

    Each part with its own format

    Projects on a clothesline of cards, Skills on a bookshelf and Contact on a pier with a lighthouse.

  3. Career

    Paper plane flight

    Walking through the door launches a paper plane, and scrolling flies it past each company.

  4. Folded paper

    3D objects

    Furniture and objects became 3D folded paper, with pencil hatching that follows the light and hand-drawn outlines.

  5. Mobile

    Free roam

    An on-screen joystick to walk the hallway on mobile.

03Technical evolution

How the paper got its depth

  1. 01

    Real-time drawing

    The pencil hatching sticks to surfaces and shifts with the light, and the outlines look hand-drawn: they overshoot corners, wobble and shake slightly, like a cartoon.

  2. 02

    Folded paper in 3D

    Furniture and objects are built from faceted surfaces and creases, like origami, instead of flat images stuck on the wall.

  3. 03

    Endless flight

    In the Career section, the sky is made of cloud chunks spawned ahead and discarded behind, so the flight can go on forever without slowing things down.

  4. 04

    Navigation for everyone

    Scroll to stroll, WASD to roam freely and an on-screen joystick on mobile.
