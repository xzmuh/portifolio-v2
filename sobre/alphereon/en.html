Alphereon

Space combat in the browser, with voxel ships, a campaign, survival mode and co-op for up to three pilots.

Why I built it

It started as a minigame hidden inside another project and grew into my lab for game design, enemy AI and multiplayer.

The experience I wanted to create

The rush of console-style space combat running right in your browser, solo or with two friends, with nothing to install.

01The original idea

Take off from the Moon and shoot

The first version fit in one sentence: take off from the Moon and shoot straight ahead down a corridor. A few minutes of gameplay.

02How it grew

From a corridor to a game you play together

  1. Oct 15

    The Moon corridor

    The hidden minigame: take off and shoot straight ahead.

  2. Oct 16

    Real space

    An open arena, a chase camera locked to the ship, layered explosions, varied enemies, the mothership, power-ups and the cockpit.

  3. Oct 20

    A game of its own

    A name, a Destiny-style orbit menu, a campaign, a hangar, ships built cube by cube and maps with their own mechanics.

  4. Oct 21

    Made to play together

    Three-pilot co-op, multi-phase bosses, weapons with personality, pilot profiles with medals, and music.

  5. Later

    Enemies that think

    Enemies stopped being targets and became pilots, with temperament, group tactics and a fight to get on your tail.

03Technical evolution

What's inside the engine

  1. 01

    Ships built in code

    Each ship is a symmetrical cube grid built from geometry and turned into two meshes: the hull, with only outer faces and per-vertex ambient occlusion, and the glowing lit parts.

  2. 02

    Multiplayer without a game server

    Co-op connects browsers directly to each other over WebRTC. One player hosts the match and the others sync to their state.

  3. 03

    Enemies that think

    Each ship runs its own cycle of moving in, passing, breaking off and repositioning, while keeping track of how many are already fighting. Temperament only tweaks the numbers in that cycle, so you get variety without breaking the game.

  4. 04

    Destructible scenery

    Debris, satellites and hives are made of plates that heat up, spin loose and burst when they take hits.

  5. 05

    Flight controls

    Gamepad support with a response curve that ramps up at full tilt, aim assist that tracks targets, and rumble.

  6. 06

    Light enough for the browser

    On-demand sound loading with a volume limiter, graphics presets and progress saved right in the browser.
